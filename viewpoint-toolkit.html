Six Foundations For Enterprise AI That Actually DeliversNew

Most AI projects stall before becoming functional business tools — not because of the technology, but because of organizational unreadiness. This piece argues that funding the tool is not enough; companies must prepare data, culture, and people first.

Key Insights

  • Clean, standardized data is the prerequisite — AI amplifies existing chaos, it doesn't fix it
  • AI that can't connect to a revenue outcome within 180 days probably isn't worth pursuing
  • Underinvestment in adoption (training, onboarding) vs. acquisition (licenses) is the most common budget mistake
  • When employees sense AI is a headcount-reduction play, they resist — reframe it as augmentation to unlock engagement
  • AI requires an ongoing feedback loop; it's a dynamic system, not a one-time deployment
  • Compliance and governance retrofitted late is far more costly than built in from the start

Inc. · Andrea Olson · May 2026 · 4 min read

Why Every Leader Needs a Better Decision-Making FrameworkNew

As leaders advance, decision complexity compounds — but most organizations hand new leaders a job description and assume the rest will sort itself out. The solution isn't a productivity tool or coaching; it's a personal operating system that shapes how you think before the moment of decision arrives.

Key Insights

  • "Acceleration without direction" is a named failure mode: saying yes to more, moving faster, staying later — feels like leadership but erodes effectiveness and credibility
  • Mentorship is episodic; coaching is reactive. Neither works in real time at 9am on a Tuesday
  • The Steering Guide framework: one guiding principle + three core commitments + supporting actions that automate good judgment over time
  • The signal it's working: someone describes how you lead — without being told — and it matches your guiding principle exactly
  • The framework evolves; the first version doesn't need to be perfect

Author

  • Useful framing for discovery: "Do your leaders have a consistent decision filter, or are they starting from scratch every time?" surfaces alignment gaps quickly
  • The principle that frameworks need to be operational (not aspirational) — built to work under pressure — reinforces the Viewpoint positioning around execution infrastructure vs. strategy documents
